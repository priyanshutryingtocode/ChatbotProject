# Refunds Policy

## Refund method

Refunds are always issued to the original payment method used at checkout.
We do not process refunds to alternate accounts or different cards.

## Refund timelines

- UPI and wallets: 24–48 hours after we approve the refund.
- Cards and net banking: 5–7 business days after approval, depending on your bank.
- Cash on delivery orders: refunded to a bank account you nominate during the
  return request; expect the amount within 5–7 business days of approval.

The timeline starts once the return passes quality check at our warehouse, not
when the carrier picks up the parcel.

## Shipping fee refunds

Original shipping fees are refunded in full when a return is due to a damaged,
defective, or incorrect item. For change-of-mind returns the original shipping
fee is not refunded.

## Cancelled orders

Orders cancelled while still in Processing status are refunded automatically
and usually appear within the timelines above, without any action needed.

## Tracking your refund

Support can confirm the exact refund status using your order number plus one
identity detail such as your registered email or phone number.
