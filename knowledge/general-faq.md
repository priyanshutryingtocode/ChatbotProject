# General FAQ

## Checking your order status

To look up any order detail we verify two things: your order number plus one
of the email, phone number, or name on the order. This protects your order
from anyone who only knows one of those details. Order numbers are four
digits, for example 0042.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed any time before an order is dispatched.
Once the order is In Transit, the address is locked with the carrier and
changes require a redelivery request after the first attempt.

## Priority levels

Orders ship at Standard priority by default. Express and Priority orders are
dispatched ahead of Standard in the same-day processing queue, but delivery
day estimates are unchanged.

## Payment statuses

Paid means payment captured successfully. Refunded means the refund has been
triggered from our side; bank posting may take a few extra days.

## Contact hours

Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM IST. The chat assistant
can answer policy questions and order-status lookups at any time.

## Common account and order questions

**What details do I need to check an order?** Provide the order number and
one matching detail from the order: its email address, phone number, or
customer name. The assistant must verify both before sharing order details.

**Can I change my delivery address?** The address can be changed before the
order is dispatched. Once it is In Transit, the address is locked with the
carrier; an address change then requires a redelivery request after the first
delivery attempt.

**Does Express or Priority shipping arrive on a different day?** Express and
Priority orders are dispatched ahead of Standard orders in the same-day
processing queue. The delivery-day estimate does not change.

**What do Paid and Refunded mean?** Paid means payment was captured
successfully. Refunded means the refund was triggered by the business; bank
posting can take additional days.

**When can I reach support?** Support is available Monday through Saturday,
9 AM–9 PM IST. The chat assistant can answer policy questions and order-status
lookups at any time.
