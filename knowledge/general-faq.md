# General FAQ

## Checking your order status

To look up any order detail we verify two things: your order number plus one
of the email, phone number, or name on the order. This protects your order
from anyone who only knows one of those details. Order numbers are four
digits, for example 0042.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed any time before an order is dispatched.
Once the order is In Transit, the address is locked with the carrier and
changes require a redelivery request after the first attempt.

## Priority levels

Orders ship at Standard priority by default. Express and Priority orders are
dispatched ahead of Standard in the same-day processing queue, but delivery
day estimates are unchanged.

## Payment statuses

Paid means payment captured successfully. Refunded means the refund has been
triggered from our side; bank posting may take a few extra days.

## Contact hours

Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM IST. The chat assistant
can answer policy questions and order-status lookups at any time.
