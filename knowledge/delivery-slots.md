# Delivery Slots

## Available slots

Where slot delivery is available you can choose one of four 3-hour windows:

- 9 AM – 12 PM
- 12 PM – 3 PM
- 3 PM – 6 PM
- 6 PM – 9 PM

The assigned slot is shown on your shipment details next to the estimated
delivery date.

## Changing your slot

Slots can be changed any time before the order status becomes Out for
Delivery. After that the driver route is fixed and the slot cannot be edited.

## Slot availability

Not every pincode supports all slots; high-demand windows fill first. If your
preferred window is unavailable, the nearest open slot is offered.

## Missed slots

If a delivery is missed during the chosen slot the order is marked as a failed
delivery attempt and reattempted automatically within 48 hours.

## Common delivery-slot questions

**How long is a delivery slot?** Each available window is three hours. The
listed windows are 9 AM–12 PM, 12 PM–3 PM, 3 PM–6 PM, and 6 PM–9 PM. Not every
window is available in every pincode.

**Can I change my delivery slot?** Yes, before the order status becomes Out
for Delivery. After that, the driver's route is fixed and the slot cannot be
edited.

**What if my preferred slot is unavailable?** Availability varies by pincode
and high-demand windows can fill. The nearest open slot is offered.

**What happens if I miss my slot?** The order is marked as a failed delivery
attempt and a reattempt is scheduled automatically within 48 hours. See Failed
Deliveries for what happens after failed attempts.
