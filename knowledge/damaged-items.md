# Damaged or Incorrect Items

## Reporting window

Report damaged, defective, or incorrect items within 48 hours of delivery.
Reports after this window are handled as standard returns under the 30-day
return policy.

## What to include

When contacting support have ready:

- Your order number.
- One identity detail — the email, phone number, or name on the order.
- Photos of the item, the packaging, and the shipping label.

## Resolution options

For confirmed damage you can choose:

1. Free replacement shipped with priority handling.
2. A full refund including the original shipping fee.

Incorrect items are always replaced at no cost; if a replacement is out of
stock a full refund including shipping is issued instead.

## No return shipment needed

For low-value damaged items (under ₹999) we usually do not require the item to
be shipped back — support confirms disposal instructions during the request.
