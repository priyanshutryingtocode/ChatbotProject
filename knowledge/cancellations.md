# Cancellations Policy

## Cancelling while processing

Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time while their status is
Processing. The refund starts automatically and follows standard refund
timelines — no request form is needed.

## After dispatch

Once an order moves to In Transit or Out for Delivery, self-cancellation is no
longer possible. You have two options:

1. Refuse the parcel at the door; the carrier returns it to us and a refund is
   initiated after the item reaches the warehouse.
2. Accept delivery and start a normal return within 30 days.

## How to cancel

Contact support with your order number plus one identity detail — the email,
phone number, or name on the order — so we can verify the request securely.

## Payment status

Cancelled orders show a Refunded payment status in the system once the refund
has been triggered, not when the money lands in your account.
