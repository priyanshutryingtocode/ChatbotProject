# Cancellations Policy

## Cancelling while processing

Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time while their status is
Processing. The refund starts automatically and follows standard refund
timelines — no request form is needed.

## After dispatch

Once an order moves to In Transit or Out for Delivery, self-cancellation is no
longer possible. You have two options:

1. Refuse the parcel at the door; the carrier returns it to us and a refund is
   initiated after the item reaches the warehouse.
2. Accept delivery and start a normal return within 30 days.

## How to cancel

Contact support with your order number plus one identity detail — the email,
phone number, or name on the order — so we can verify the request securely.

## Payment status

Cancelled orders show a Refunded payment status in the system once the refund
has been triggered, not when the money lands in your account.

## Common cancellation questions

**Can I cancel an order that has not shipped yet?** An order can be cancelled
free of charge while its status is Processing. Once it is In Transit or Out
for Delivery, self-cancellation is no longer available.

**What if the parcel is already with the carrier?** You can refuse the parcel
at the door and the carrier will return it; the refund is initiated after it
reaches the warehouse. Or accept delivery and start a normal return within 30
days. See the Returns Policy for return conditions and fees.

**Do I need to fill out a cancellation form?** No request form is needed for
an order still in Processing. Contact support with the order number and one
matching identity detail (email, phone, or name) so the request can be
verified.

**Does a Refunded status mean the money is already in my account?** No. It
means the refund has been triggered in the order system. The time for funds to
appear depends on the refund timeline in the Refunds Policy.
