# Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines

Standard delivery takes 2–7 business days from dispatch depending on your
pincode. Metro cities are usually served in 2–3 days; remote pincodes can take
the full week.

## Carriers and Courier Companies

Courier company and carrier mean the same thing in this policy. We ship with Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, Ecom Express, and XpressBees. The
carrier assigned depends on your pincode and is shown on the shipment details
alongside a tracking number.

## Tracking numbers

Tracking numbers start with IN followed by eight digits, for example
IN00000042. Tracking becomes active a few hours after the order is dispatched.

## Shipping charges

- Free shipping on orders of ₹499 or more.
- Below ₹499, a flat fee between ₹49 and ₹149 applies based on weight zone.

## Delivery slots

Where slot-based delivery is available you can pick a 3-hour window such as
9 AM – 12 PM, 12 PM – 3 PM, 3 PM – 6 PM, or 6 PM – 9 PM.
