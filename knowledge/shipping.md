# Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines

Standard delivery takes 2–7 business days from dispatch depending on your
pincode. Metro cities are usually served in 2–3 days; remote pincodes can take
the full week.

## Carriers and Courier Companies

Courier company and carrier mean the same thing in this policy. We ship with Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, Ecom Express, and XpressBees. The
carrier assigned depends on your pincode and is shown on the shipment details
alongside a tracking number.

## Tracking numbers

Tracking numbers start with IN followed by eight digits, for example
IN00000042. Tracking becomes active a few hours after the order is dispatched.

## Shipping charges

- Free shipping on orders of ₹499 or more.
- Below ₹499, a flat fee between ₹49 and ₹149 applies based on weight zone.

## Delivery slots

Where slot-based delivery is available you can pick a 3-hour window such as
9 AM – 12 PM, 12 PM – 3 PM, 3 PM – 6 PM, or 6 PM – 9 PM.

**How long does standard shipping take?** It takes 2–7 business days from
dispatch, depending on the pincode. Metro cities usually take 2–3 days;
remote pincodes can take the full week.

Blue Dart, DTDC, Ecom Express, and XpressBees. The assigned carrier depends on
the pincode and appears in the shipment details with the tracking number.

**When will tracking work, and what does the number look like?** A tracking
number starts with `IN` followed by eight digits, for example `IN00000042`.
Tracking becomes active a few hours after dispatch.

**When is shipping free?** Orders of ₹499 or more have free shipping. Below
₹499, the shipping fee is ₹49–₹149 depending on the weight zone.

**Are delivery windows available everywhere?** Slot delivery is available
only where supported. Available windows are three hours; see Delivery Slots
for the windows, availability, and change rules.
