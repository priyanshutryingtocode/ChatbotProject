# Failed Deliveries

## What the status means

A Failed Delivery status means the carrier attempted delivery but could not
complete it — common reasons include nobody at the address, inaccessible
gates, or incorrect contact details.

## Automatic reattempts

The carrier automatically reattempts within 48 hours. You do not need to book
a redelivery; make sure someone is available at the address during the chosen
delivery slot.

## Attempt limit

After two failed attempts the parcel returns to our warehouse and a full
refund is initiated automatically to the original payment method.

## Preventing a failure

Before the next attempt you can:

- Confirm the address and landmark with support.
- Switch to a more convenient 3-hour delivery slot.
- Ask a neighbour or security desk to accept the parcel on your behalf.

Support can update these details using your order number plus one identity
detail such as your registered email or phone number.
