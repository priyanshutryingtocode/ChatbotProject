# Returns Policy

## Return window

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The 30-day window starts on
the date the order is marked Delivered in our system, not the date you receive
the notification email.

## Condition requirements

To be eligible for a return, items must be unused and in their original
packaging with all tags and accessories included. We may reject returns where
the product shows signs of use beyond a basic opening check.

## Non-returnable categories

For hygiene reasons, opened personal-care products cannot be returned unless
they arrived damaged or defective.

## How to initiate a return

Contact support with your order number and one identity detail — the email,
phone number, or name on the order — so we can locate it securely. Support will
share a prepaid return label; drop the parcel at any partner pickup point of
the carrier noted on the label.

## Return shipping charges

Return pickup is free for damaged or wrong items. For change-of-mind returns,
a flat fee of ₹99 is deducted from the refund.

## Common return questions

**How long do I have to return an item?** The return window is 30 days from
the date the order is marked Delivered in the system. The email-notification
date does not start the window.

**Does the item need to be unused and in its original packaging?** Yes. It
must be unused, with original packaging, tags, and accessories. A basic
opening check is distinguished from signs of use beyond it; the latter can
lead to rejection.

**Can I return opened personal-care products?** Opened personal-care items
cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they arrived damaged or
defective.

**How do I send a return?** Contact support with the order number and one
matching identity detail (email, phone, or name). Support shares a prepaid
label; drop the parcel at a partner pickup point for the carrier on that
label.

**Is return pickup free?** Pickup is free for damaged or wrong items. For a
change-of-mind return, ₹99 is deducted from the refund. The original shipping
fee is not refunded for a change-of-mind return; see the Refunds Policy for
shipping-fee rules on damaged or incorrect items.
